- **The ONE dream customer:** a 40–60-year-old local who *used* to feel energetic and now feels flat — poor sleep, low energy, a body that aches and won't bounce back like it did. They're not sick enough for the doctor but not well enough to feel themselves. (Secondary avatar: the active/athlete who wants faster recovery — same offer, different angle.)
- **Pain, in their words:** "I'm tired all the time." "I wake up stiff." "It takes me forever to recover." "I feel old before my time."
- **Dream outcome:** wake up with energy, move without the aches, recover fast, and feel a decade younger — so they can keep up with life, training, and family.
- **Transformation:** From *running on empty, aching, slow to recover* → to *energised, mobile, recovering like they're younger*.
- **Why unsolved:** they've tried supplements, more sleep, "pushing through," maybe physio — nothing addresses recovery at the **cellular** level, which is your angle.
