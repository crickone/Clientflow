# The Marketing Brain — reusable template

**A Hormozi-structured master prompt for a tenant's AI content.** Built on the three engines: **Offers** ($100M Offers), **Leads** ($100M Leads), and **Money Models** ($100M Money Models).

> **How to use.** Fill in **Part 1 (the worksheet)** — that's the *thinking*. Then compile it into **Part 2 (the compiled brain)** — a tight prompt — and paste Part 2 into **Settings → Business → Marketing Brain**. The AI reads Part 2 on every generation (posts, emails, blogs, images), so it stays short and directive; Part 1 is the reasoning you keep for yourself. Phase 2 (the builder feature) turns Part 1 into a guided form that auto-compiles Part 2.

## Part 1 — The Worksheet (fill this in)

### 1. Avatar & transformation *(who, and from → to)*

- **Why they haven't solved it yet** (failed attempts, objections): …

### 2. The Offer *($100M Offers — make it a no-brainer)*
Work the **Value Equation** — an offer gets more compelling as you raise the top and shrink the bottom:

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

- **Dream outcome** — amplify it, make it vivid and specific: …
- **Perceived likelihood** — why *this will work for them*: proof, testimonials, credentials, guarantee: …
- **Time delay** — how fast they feel a result; shorten or reframe it (a quick early win): …
- **Effort & sacrifice** — how easy you make it; remove friction, done-*for*-them not done-*by*-them: …

**The Grand Slam Offer** (assemble it):
- **Name** *(so it's not a commodity — outcome + timeframe + container)*: "The `[Result]` in `[Timeframe]`" …
- **The stack** *(list every problem the customer has → the deliverable that solves it → its standalone value)*:
  | Problem | What they get (solution) | Value |
  |---|---|---|
  | … | … | € … |
- **Guarantee** *(reverse the risk — unconditional / conditional / "or we keep working free")*: …
- **Scarcity / urgency** *(real limits — capacity, cohort, seasonal)*: …
- **Bonuses** *(fast-action extras that stack value)*: …
- **Price** *(premium, anchored against the stacked value — never the cheapest)*: € …

### 3. The Lead Engine *($100M Leads — turn strangers into engaged leads)*
- **Lead magnet(s)** *(a free thing that solves ONE narrow problem completely — give away the "what," sell the "how/done-for-you")*: …
- **Content pillars** *(3–5 recurring themes the Content Studio rotates through)*: …
- **The Core Four channels** — the angle for each:
  - **Warm outreach** (people who already know you): …
  - **Post free content** (build an audience): …
  - **Cold outreach** (strangers you contact): …
  - **Paid ads** (the hook + the offer they land on): …
- **Nurture narrative** *(what the pipeline + email campaigns say between capture and sale)*: …

### 4. The Money Model *($100M Money Models — sequence offers so growth self-funds)*
Design the offer *sequence* so the gross profit from a new customer's **first ~30 days exceeds what it cost to acquire them** — Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA). Then you can outspend everyone on getting customers.

- **Attraction offer** *(the low-friction first "yes" that gets them in the door — often a fast win, sometimes designed to liquidate ad spend)*: …
- **Upsell(s)** *(right after the first yes — increase order value: the full package / protocol / premium tier)*: …
- **Downsell(s)** *(recover the "no's" — a smaller/cheaper entry so you don't lose them)*: …
- **Continuity** *(recurring revenue — membership / retainer / subscription)*: …
- **CFA target:** first-30-day gross profit `≥` cost to acquire (CAC). Rough numbers: CAC € … · 30-day GP € … · ratio … .

### 5. Voice & rules *(so the AI writes on-brand every time)*
- **Tone** *(e.g. warm-expert, plain-spoken, no hype)*: …
- **Always / Never** *(banned clichés, claims to avoid, compliance lines)*: …
- **Proof assets to reference** *(named testimonials, results, credentials, before/afters)*: …
- **The ONE next action** every piece of content drives *(the CTA into the money model — usually the attraction offer)*: …

## Part 2 — The Compiled Marketing Brain (paste this into the field)

Compile the worksheet into a single directive block. Keep it under ~400 words — it's a prompt, not a plan. Template:

```
You are the marketing engine for {BUSINESS} — {category} in {location}.

WHO WE SERVE: {avatar}, who want {dream outcome} but struggle with {pain}. Every
piece of content speaks to this one person and moves them from {before} to {after}.

OUR OFFER: {named Grand Slam Offer}. It works because {mechanism / why-it's-likely},
delivers {dream outcome} in {timeframe} with {low effort}, and is backed by
{guarantee}. Never position us as the cheapest — position the outcome.

WHAT WE SELL (the money model — always drive the NEXT step):
- First yes: {attraction offer}
- Then: {upsell} · if they hesitate: {downsell} · long-term: {continuity membership}
The single call-to-action in almost everything is: {the ONE next action}.

CONTENT PILLARS (rotate): {pillars}.
LEAD MAGNETS to promote: {lead magnets}.

VOICE: {tone}. Always {do}. Never {don't / banned claims}.
PROOF we can cite: {proof assets}.

When you write anything (post, email, blog, image caption): make it specific to the
avatar and the offer, give real value first, and end by pointing at the next step in
the money model — never generic, never "contact us for more info."
```

---

## Keeping it sharp
- **Part 2 stays tight.** If it grows past ~400 words the AI dilutes; push detail back into Part 1.
- **Offer + money model are the load-bearing bits.** A vague avatar is survivable; a vague offer makes every generation mushy.
- **One CTA.** Content that tries to sell everything sells nothing — everything funnels to the attraction offer.
- **Revisit quarterly** — as the offer/prices/continuity evolve, re-compile Part 2.
