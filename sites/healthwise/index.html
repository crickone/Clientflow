The studio

One room in Clonmel, built for this.

Healthwise opened in 2011 as an exercise and lifestyle management studio, and has run the same way since: small groups, a coach in the room for every session, and a programme written for the person rather than for the class.

Behind it is thirty years of coaching — An Garda Síochána and the Garda College, an MSc in Performance Coaching, certification in cardiac rehabilitation, and squads at Munster Rugby and Clonmel Commercials — pointed at ordinary people rather than at athletes.
