What a session actually is

Light weights, bands, bikes and treadmills. A coach in the room the whole time.

A Vitality class is small and quiet. You work on the machines and with light dumbbells and resistance bands, at a pace set for you, with a coach watching and adjusting. Nobody is left to guess. Nobody is pushed past what is sensible.

Over the weeks the aim is simple: more lean muscle, so daily things get easier; better balance, so a slip is less likely to become a fall; and more confidence and energy than you walked in with.

Who comes

This is for you if any of these sound familiar.

You have never exercised

Most people in the room started here. The first weeks are about learning the movements and finding your level, nothing more.

Your mobility is limited

Seated work, supported movements and a room laid out so you can get to everything. Bring the walking stick; it is welcome.

You are coming back after a health event

A hip, a knee, a spell in hospital, a cardiac procedure. We start from where you are now, and if a cardiac event is part of your story, Heartwise may be the better door.

You want company as much as fitness

The classes are social by design. People stay for the chat, and the chat is half of why they keep coming.

Build

Strength and stamina. Balance and mobility. Confidence and energy.
