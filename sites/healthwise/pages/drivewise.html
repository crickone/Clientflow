The programme

Four parts, tailored to the company.

Wellness screening

Body composition, hydration, sleep and cardiovascular checks for each driver.

Functional movement testing

Grip strength, posture and aerobic fitness, with the ergonomics of the driving seat in mind.

Classroom education

Legal compliance, fatigue management and defensive driving.

On-road evaluation

Road, classroom, road again: anticipation, hazard identification and reaction time, coached by an advanced Garda driving instructor.
