The coach

DJ O'Dwyer

DJ opened Healthwise in 2011 after thirty years in An Garda Síochána, five of them teaching physical studies at the Garda College. He holds an MSc in Performance Coaching and is certified in cardiac rehabilitation, and he has coached strength and conditioning for Munster Rugby's development squads, Tipperary minor football, and Clonmel Commercials.

The programmes here are what that experience looks like when it is pointed at ordinary people: careful, specific, and honest about what takes time.
