The gap this fills

Rehab is seven weeks. The rest of your life is longer.

After a stent, a bypass or a cardiac event, the hospital programme gets you moving again, and then it ends. Most people are told to keep exercising and sent home to work out how. Heartwise is the room to do it in: coached, monitored, with people who have been through the same thing.

Heartwise is run by a coach certified in cardiac rehabilitation with the British Association for Cardiovascular Prevention and Rehabilitation. Sessions start gently, progress carefully, and pay attention to how you are on the day.

What Heartwise includes

Exercise, food, and the habits around them.

Coached group exercise

Strength, balance and gentle cardio on the machines, tailored to your history and progressed as you are ready. Never more people than the coach can watch.

Nutrition, kept simple

A plan built around your life rather than a diet sheet, and a food diary we look at together so the changes hold.

Diabetes and blood pressure

Regular exercise supports insulin sensitivity and healthy blood pressure. We coach the movement and the habits around it, alongside your GP's care, never instead of it.

Confidence back

Trust in your body is rebuilt the same way strength is: gradually, with steady wins, and with someone paying attention.

Bring your discharge letter and your questions. We'll take it from there.
