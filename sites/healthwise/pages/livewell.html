Who it is for

People who have noticed the change and want to get ahead of it.

Livewell is for anyone aged 40 to 60 who is starting to feel the early signs: stiffness in the morning, less energy in the afternoon, weight that is harder to shift than it used to be, a back that complains after a day at a desk or in a van.

Nothing here is extreme. Every class is coached, the loads are set for you, and the point is to build the strength, mobility and cardiovascular fitness that keep you independent and sharp for the next thirty years, not to leave you flattened for the next three days.
