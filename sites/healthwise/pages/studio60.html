What a session actually is

Real training, sized for the person doing it.

Studio 60 is for people over 60 who are already moving well and want more than a gentle class. Sessions use free weights and the machines, with conditioning work alongside, and the load goes up as you get stronger.

The group stays small and a coach is in the room throughout, watching the movement and adjusting the weight. Nothing here is a class you follow from the back row.

{{RULE}}

Who comes

This is for you if any of these sound familiar.

You already train, and you want it coached

You walk, you cycle, you have used a gym before. What you want is a programme and somebody watching the technique, not a room full of machines and a guess.

You want to lift properly

Real weight, progressed sensibly. Strength is the thing that holds off most of what people fear about getting older, and it responds at any age.

A gentle class is not enough

If you have been finding the easier classes comfortable rather than challenging, this is the room for you. If you are starting from scratch or your mobility is limited, Vitality 60+ is the better door.

You want to keep doing what you do

Golf, hills, the garden, lifting grandchildren. The point of the training is the life outside it, and the programme is written around what you want to keep.

Build

Strength and power. Bone and muscle. Stamina that lasts the day.
