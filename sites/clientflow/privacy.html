Data we collect

Account and business data. When you sign up, book a demo or contact us, we collect your name, business name, email, phone number, business type, billing details and login credentials.

Member and client data (processed on behalf of gyms and clinics). When you use ClientFlow to run your business, you upload personal data about your members and clients — names, contact details, bookings, attendance, memberships, packages, payment records and notes. We process this only to provide the service to you, under your instructions.

Payment data. Card and payment details are collected and processed by our payment processor (see Processors we use). We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.

Usage and analytics data. With your consent where required, we collect analytics about how our website and platform are used — pages viewed, device and browser type, and general location derived from IP. Non-essential analytics run only after you consent via our cookie banner.

Cookies. We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you logged in and secure, and — with consent — analytics cookies. See Cookies below.

Communications. If you connect an email inbox (e.g. Gmail) or message clients through the platform, we process the content of those communications to deliver the messaging features you've enabled.

Lawful bases

We rely on the following GDPR lawful bases:

  • Contract — to provide the platform, manage your subscription and support you.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure our systems, prevent fraud, and improve the product, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — for non-essential analytics cookies and certain marketing communications. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — to meet accounting, tax and other legal requirements.

For member and client data you upload, your lawful basis as controller is your responsibility; we process it under our data processing terms with you.

Processors we use

We share personal data only with vetted sub-processors who help us run the service. Current processors include:

  • Railway (hosting, EU-West region) — application and database hosting.
  • Anthropic (AI processing) — powers AI marketing and assistant features. Content sent for AI processing is used to generate your output and is not used to train models under our terms.
  • Resend (email delivery) — sending transactional and marketing email.
  • Google / Gmail (where you connect it) — to provide two-way email inbox features you enable.
  • Payment processor — CreatePay / Cardstream — to process card payments securely.

We put appropriate data processing agreements and safeguards in place with each. Where any transfer outside the EEA occurs, we rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Account data — for the life of your subscription and up to 7 years afterward where required for tax and accounting.
  • Member and client data — kept while you use the platform; on termination we return or delete it per our terms and your instructions.
  • Analytics data — retained in aggregated or limited form per our analytics settings.
  • Support and communications — kept as long as needed to resolve queries and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to: access your data; correct inaccurate data; erase data ("right to be forgotten"); restrict or object to processing; data portability; and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or your local supervisory authority.

If you are a member or client of a gym or clinic that uses ClientFlow, please direct requests to that business first — they are the controller of your data. We will assist them in responding.

To exercise your rights against us as controller, contact privacy@clientflow.ie (placeholder).

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies (login, security and session) that don't require consent, and — only where you accept our cookie banner — a small number of analytics cookies. Clearing your cookies for this site will show the banner again, so you can change your mind at any time.

Changes to this policy
