FAQ

The questions everyone asks first.

Is there really no contract?
Correct. Month to month. Give notice anytime and you're free to go.
What does migration cost?
Nothing. We move your members, classes and history from your old tool for free.
Is VAT included in the €200?
Price is quoted ex-VAT. Irish VAT is added per standard rules on your invoice.
What are the card payment fees?
Around 40% below Stripe, with no marketplace commission on your clients. Exact rate shown on the demo.
Are there setup or onboarding fees?
No. Setup, migration and building your website are all included in the €200.
What if I add more members or classes?
Nothing changes. €200 is flat — you're not billed per member or per booking.
Will the price go up later?
No surprise hikes. We built this brand on the opposite of that. Any change is opt-in.
What if I want to leave?
You export your data and your site content — you own both. We help you off, no hostage-taking.
Do I need technical skills?
No. We build the website and brand the app for you. You run your business, not the software.
