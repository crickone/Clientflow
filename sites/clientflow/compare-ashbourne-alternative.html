The verdict

Two honest sentences.

Ashbourne has been collecting membership dues across Ireland and the UK since 1997, and its in-house SEPA/BACS Direct Debit collection with arrears-chasing is genuinely proven — a big reason clubs stay loyal to it.

But it's a closed, dated system with zero AI and zero third-party integrations, fully opaque pricing, and a collections-agency brand that can appear right on your members' bank statements — its website is an optional add-on, not a real product.
