About ClientFlow

We built this for our own clients first. Then we opened the doors.

ClientFlow didn't start as software. It started as an agency running marketing for gyms and wellness clinics around Ireland. We built their websites, filled their classes, chased their leads and watched their bills — and we kept hitting the same wall: the tools they paid for were expensive, splintered across five vendors, quick to hike prices, and none of them could give a client a real website. So we built our own platform to run our clients' businesses properly. It worked. Owners kept asking if they could just have the thing we'd built. Eventually we said yes, and productised it. That's ClientFlow.

Why flat pricing

€200, flat, forever-honest — on purpose.

We've sat on the customer side of the add-on trap. We've watched "starter" prices quietly become €400 bills, and month-to-month promises turn into contracts. So flat, published, month-to-month pricing isn't a marketing angle for us — it's a reaction to everything we hated. One price. Everything on. Cancel whenever. If we ever have to raise it, you'll opt in, not get ambushed.
