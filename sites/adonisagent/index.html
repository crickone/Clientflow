BEFORE YOU BOOK

Questions we
get asked.

Is AdonisAgent right for my business?

It is built for gyms, fitness studios, clinics and wellness businesses. The demo is where you check it against your own workflows and decide whether it fits.

How much control do I have over the AI?

An agent prepares work and proposes an action. Every change it wants to make comes back to you first: you see what it would do, and it only runs if you approve it.

Can I bring my existing client information?

Yes, the platform imports clients. In the demo we go through your current system, what needs to move, and which integrations are available.

What does it cost?

We go through pricing, what is included and any usage-based costs in the demo, so you know the full figure before you decide.

What happens when I request a demo?

Leave your name and email, and your business name if you want. We email you to arrange a 20-minute walkthrough. The form starts a conversation: it does not create an account or sign you up to anything.
