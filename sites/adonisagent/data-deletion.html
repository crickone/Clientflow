Deleting your data

How to have Vantaige Limited, the company behind AdonisAgent, delete the data it holds from a connected Facebook Page or Instagram account, or any other personal data. The full privacy policy explains what we collect and why.

Last updated 3 October 2026

How to request deletion

Facebook and Instagram. To remove the data AdonisAgent holds from a connected Facebook Page or Instagram account:

  1. In AdonisAgent, go to Settings → Integrations → Facebook and disconnect the Page. This deletes the stored access token straight away.
  2. Optionally, also remove AdonisAgent on Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
  3. To have posts, post history and leads brought in from Facebook deleted from AdonisAgent too, email privacy@adonisagent.ie with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name of the Page. Posts already published on Facebook or Instagram stay there; you can delete those in Facebook or Instagram.

Everything else. Email privacy@adonisagent.ie to have your account or enquiry data deleted. If you are a member or client of a business that uses AdonisAgent, ask that business; we will act on its instructions.

We confirm deletion requests by email and complete them within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law.
