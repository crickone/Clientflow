Four agents. One operating system.

Not another dashboard to check. A team that does the work — supervised by you, never off the leash.

A/ SALES

Fills the schedule

Replies to every new lead in seconds, then follows up until they book — across email and WhatsApp.

  • Instant first reply to enquiries
  • Relentless multi-touch follow-up
  • Books tours & trials into your calendar
B/ MARKETING

Keeps you visible

Drafts and schedules the posts, writes the blog, asks happy members for reviews — in your gym's voice.

  • AI blog + social, on schedule
  • Review requests to the right members
  • Runs your Marketing Brain
C/ COMMS

Never leaves them waiting

Answers member questions, triages the inbox, and re-books no-shows before they churn.

  • WhatsApp + email, one inbox
  • No-show & re-engagement nudges
  • Escalates only what needs you

Guards the cash

Chases failed payments, reminds the overdue, and reconciles the takings — plugging the leak most gyms never see.

  • Auto-retry failed direct debits
  • Dunning on overdue memberships
  • Nightly reconciliation
