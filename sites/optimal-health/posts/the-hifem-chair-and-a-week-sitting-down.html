The HIFEM chair, and a week spent sitting down

Long days sitting leave your deep muscles doing very little. What the chair does, and why you plan it like a gym session.

What a sitting week leaves behind

Eight hours at a desk, a commute and an evening on the sofa. Hips and lower back tighten, shoulders round forward, and the deep muscles that hold you upright do very little all day.

The pelvic floor is one of the hardest of these to work on purpose: many people can't feel it well enough to train it accurately.

A contraction you didn't have to make

The chair creates a focused magnetic field that passes through clothing and skin and makes the muscle contract. That's why you keep your clothes on.

It can produce thousands of contractions in a session, more than you could make on your own.

Twenty to thirty minutes, in what you arrived in

You sit down, we set the intensity with you, and the session runs twenty to thirty minutes. You feel a firm, rhythmic pulsing in the muscle underneath you. Most people talk, read or just sit. There's nothing to change into, and nothing is internal, including the pelvic floor work.

Plan it like a gym session

Unlike the bed and the chamber, the chair is muscle work. Don't stack it on top of a hard session, and don't try it the day before something important until you know how you respond. Many people put it on a light day or after their main session.

Judge it over weeks

Like any muscle work, one session won't shift much, and a few weeks of them might. Book a single session first to see how you find it.
