## Read this before you place anything

**The claims rule.** The clinic's reception wall carries printed boards reading
*"Accelerates muscle repair"*, *"Increases oxygen delivery for faster recovery"*,
*"Enhances stamina and performance capacity"*, *"Improves mental focus and reaction
time"*, *"Recharges cellular energy for peak performance"* and similar. This website
exists to not make those claims. Every image in this manifest has been checked at
full resolution and **no file here carries a legible outcome claim.** Where a frame
had one, it was either cropped before it was written (`lounge-sofa.jpg`) or rejected
outright (see "Rejected" at the bottom). You do not need to crop anything in this
list. If you go back to the source library yourself, check every frame again.

**What signage does appear, and is fine.** Room straplines are painted on the walls
and doors and are unavoidable in any wide shot of the space:
"HEAT. REPAIR. RESET." (infrared), "BOOST. RECOVER. PERFORM." (hyperbaric),
"RECHARGE. BALANCE. STRENGTHEN." (the chair). These name a therapy; they do not
assert a health outcome. They are noted per file below.

**The naming conflict.** The clinic's own door signage says **"PEMF THERAPY"**.
This site never uses the word PEMF — it is the HIFEM chair. Two files show that
door label legibly (`waiting-room-doors.jpg`, and the pre-existing `room.jpg`).
Do not put either of them next to body copy that names the therapy, or the picture
will contradict the words beside it. They are fine as ambience further down a page.

## Consent and the photography gap — operator decisions, 2026-09-30

**Client faces are cleared for publication.** The operator confirmed on
2026-09-30 that consent is covered for the six frames showing identifiable
clients (four hyperbaric, two HIFEM chair). They may be placed. The `face:`
column below stays as a record of which frames those are, so the decision can be
revisited per image rather than re-derived.

**No photo shoot.** The operator's decision is to ship with these 28. That means:

- **Hyperbaric leans on equipment and room frames.** It is the flagship therapy
  and the thinnest set in the library: five usable frames, no photograph of the
  empty chamber exists anywhere.
- **Two files show a treatment door signed "PEMF THERAPY"** (`waiting-room-doors.jpg`
  and the pre-existing `room.jpg`). This site never uses that name -- the machine
  is the HIFEM chair. Neither file may sit next to copy naming the therapy.
- **The site depends on a CSS crop for claims safety.** `.shot--room` keeps the
  reception wall boards out of frame on every use of `reception.jpg`. Thirty
  further frames were rejected because those boards are legible in them. If a
  shoot ever happens, two reception angles that simply keep the boards out of
  shot would remove that dependency.

**Faces.** A client's face needs consent this project does not have. Files are
marked `face: clear` where someone is plainly identifiable. Prefer the anonymous
ones. If you use a marked one, the operator should confirm that person consented.
Staff faces (the therapist in the blue tunic) are marked separately — she is an
employee, which is a different question, but still worth the operator's nod.

## How these were processed

Originals untouched. Two passes per file:

1. `ffmpeg` re-encode at full resolution to bake the EXIF orientation into the
   pixels and strip all metadata (camera, timestamps, any GPS).
2. `sips` resize and JPEG compress, with quality stepped down per file until it hit
   a 180 KB budget, floored at quality 58 so nothing visibly breaks up.

Long edge 1600px for landscape frames, 1200px for square and portrait ones — a
1600px image still covers an 800px slot on a 2x display, which is the right size
for in-page imagery. The set averages 167 KB per file, against 546 KB for the
pre-existing `reception.jpg`. Every file was decode-tested after compression and a
sample was opened and looked at.
