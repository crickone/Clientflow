What it is

More oxygen, where your body needs it.

As your cells take in higher levels of oxygen, everyday fatigue starts to fade, focus sharpens, and recovery feels easier — not just from workouts, but from life.

Most people describe a calm clarity and steadier energy that lasts well beyond the session itself.

Practicalities

A session, start to finish.

01

02

03

You sit for the session. Read, listen to something, or close your eyes.

04

You put your shoes on and go back to your day. There is no recovery from the recovery.
