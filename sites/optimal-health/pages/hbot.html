Oxygen under gentle pressure.

That is most of the idea. You sit in a clear, comfortable chamber and breathe air with a higher concentration of oxygen than the room has, while the pressure inside is raised slowly over the first few minutes. You breathe through a soft, comfortable mask, and that is the only thing attached to you. Most people notice the pressure the way they would on a plane coming in to land, a soft fullness in the ears that clears when they swallow, and then stop noticing it for the rest of the hour.

At room pressure almost all the oxygen you breathe is carried by red blood cells, and those cells are close to full already, so breathing harder does not add much. Pressure changes that. It dissolves oxygen directly into the plasma, the fluid those cells travel in, so your blood carries more of it than it otherwise could and can bring it further into tissue. Oxygen is the fuel the body repairs itself with. A session is simply an hour of having more of it to hand than usual.

One session is one session. What builds is the series. Given more oxygen more often, the body can also respond by growing new capillaries — the smallest blood vessels, the ones that reach where the larger ones do not — and by laying down stronger collagen, the protein that gives skin, tendon and connective tissue their structure.

What people ask before their first session.

Is this a medical treatment?

No. Hospitals and accredited clinics use higher-pressure hyperbaric oxygen for specific medical indications, under a doctor's care. What we offer is mild hyperbaric oxygen for general wellness goals: energy, clarity, sleep, and feeling more ready between training or busy days. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

What is hyperbaric oxygen therapy?

A calm, guided session in a pressurised chamber, breathing air with a higher concentration of oxygen than the room has. The gentle pressure helps your body take on more oxygen than usual, which may support steadier energy, a clearer head, better sleep and day-to-day recovery.

At Optimal Health and Recovery we offer mild hyperbaric oxygen for general wellness only. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

Who is it suitable for?

Busy professionals, parents, people over forty building strength, and desk workers who want a calm hour that supports energy, focus, sleep routine and everyday ease. If you have specific medical concerns, speak with your GP first.

How long is a session, and how often should I come?

A session here runs forty-five to sixty minutes. Most people start with one or two a week for three to four weeks, then adjust around how they feel and what the week looks like.

What does it feel like inside the chamber?

Most people describe it as quiet and relaxing. At the start you will notice gentle pressure in your ears, similar to an aircraft landing. We will show you an easy equalising technique and stay in touch throughout, so you are comfortable the whole way.

What if I feel claustrophobic?

We can arrange a familiarisation sit-in with no pressure at all, so you can see the space and practise equalising before you commit to a session. You can talk to us at any point once you are inside, and we can pause or depressurise if needed.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable everyday clothes, and arrive hydrated. For safety in an oxygen-rich environment, leave off oily lotions, hairsprays and perfumes just before your session. A book is fine. Electronics are not, so leave your phone and your headphones with us.

Can I bring a book or my phone in?

A book is fine, and plenty of people bring one. Electronics are not: no phone, no headphones, no tablet, no e-reader. That is a safety rule rather than a preference about how you spend the hour. The air in the chamber is oxygen-rich, which is the same reason we ask you to leave off oily lotions and hairspray. Leave anything electronic with us at the desk and we will keep it safe.

Plenty of people bring nothing at all and simply rest or nap, which is what we would gently suggest for a first session.

Will I notice anything after the first session?

It varies from person to person. Some people feel clearer and more settled after the first visit; others notice sleep or energy change after a few. We keep short check-ins on energy, sleep and general ease, so you can decide for yourself whether the rhythm is working.

What should I do afterwards?

Hydrate, move gently, and notice how your energy and sleep feel over the next twenty-four hours. There is no downtime. Most people go straight back to work or on with the day.

Is it safe, and are there side effects?

Run correctly, mild hyperbaric oxygen is generally considered safe. Possible effects include temporary ear or sinus pressure, mild ear discomfort if equalising is difficult, and occasionally short-lived shifts in vision. We supervise every session, brief you before you begin, and stop or adjust if anything feels off.

Who should check with a GP before booking?

If you are pregnant or trying to conceive, have ear or sinus problems, difficulty equalising pressure, heart or respiratory conditions, implanted medical devices, recent surgery, or significant claustrophobia, please consult your GP before using hyperbaric oxygen.

Are there people who should avoid it altogether?

If you have had a recent pneumothorax, certain ear surgeries, or have devices or conditions that are not compatible with pressure changes, hyperbaric oxygen may not be suitable for you. Share your health history with us; we may ask for GP clearance before going ahead.

Can I come while pregnant?

Please get advice from your GP. We do not proceed without appropriate clearance.

Can I come with a cold or sinus congestion?

Better to reschedule. Congestion makes equalising uncomfortable. Wait until you are fully better, and check with your GP if you are unsure.

Can I train on the same day?

Yes. Plenty of people book the chamber in the morning for a clear head and train later, or train first and book infrared in the evening to wind down. We will help you place sessions so you feel ready rather than sluggish.

Can I combine it with the other therapies?

Yes, and most regulars do. A common week is hyperbaric oxygen in the morning for focus and infrared in the evening for wind-down and sleep, with HIFEM or a massage placed where they suit you. Tell us what your week looks like and we will space them sensibly.

What is the best way to start?

Book a single session, or ring us first on 083 867 2844 if you would rather talk it through. We will confirm it suits you, suggest timing — mornings for a clear day, evenings for sleep — and plan your first week so you know exactly what to expect.
