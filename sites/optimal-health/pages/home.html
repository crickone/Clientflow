Who it is for

About the clinic

Built for real people: training hard, living busy, or simply wanting to move, sleep and feel better again.

Our sessions support your body's natural processes, so you can keep up with life. You do not need to be an athlete, and you do not need to know which therapy you want before you arrive.

Between twelve minutes and an hour, and nothing to rest off afterwards, so most people fit a session into a working day. The HIFEM chair is the one exception. That is muscle work, and it is worth planning the way you would plan a gym session.

The therapies

See all four

You sit in a pressurised chamber and breathe air with a higher concentration of oxygen than the room has. It is the pressure that does the work, not the percentage on the tank. Under it, oxygen dissolves further into the blood than it can at room pressure. Most people read, rest, or doze.

See hyperbaric oxygen

Deep, gentle warmth from an infrared bed, reaching muscle and joint rather than only warming the skin. You lie down fully clothed and the bed does the rest. The bed itself runs twelve minutes, so the whole visit sits inside half an hour.

See infrared

High-Intensity Focused Electromagnetic fields, applied while you sit on the chair in whatever you arrived in. What you feel is a firm, rhythmic pulsing in the muscle underneath you, and the intensity is set with you rather than at you. It can work muscle that is genuinely hard to find on purpose, the pelvic floor most of all.

See hifem

Ten treatments, all of them hands-on and all of them carried out by a clinical therapist. Three are disciplines in their own right — therapeutic massage, reflexology and lymphatic drainage — and the rest are variations on the table: a different pressure, a different set of tools, a different length of hour. Thirty minutes to an hour, taken on their own or alongside the other therapies.
