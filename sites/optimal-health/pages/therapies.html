Common questions

Before you come in.

Do I need to be an athlete?

No. Most of the people who come in are not. The clinic was built for people training hard, living busy, or simply wanting to move and sleep better.

What do I wear?

Whatever you arrived in. You stay fully clothed for HIFEM and for hyperbaric oxygen. For infrared, comfortable clothing is easiest.

How long does a session take?

Between twelve minutes and an hour depending on the therapy. There is nothing to recover from afterwards, so you can come on a lunch break — with the HIFEM chair the exception, since that one is muscle work and is better planned like a gym session.

Do I have to buy a block?

No. Start with a single session. Blocks work out cheaper if you decide to keep going, and every price is on one page.

Can I ask questions first?

Please do. Call 083 867 2844 or send a message and we will tell you honestly whether we think it suits what you are after.
