Common questions

Common questions.

Do I need to be an athlete?

No. Most people who come in aren't.

What do I wear, and do I have to undress?

It depends on the treatment. For HIFEM and hyperbaric oxygen you stay fully clothed, in whatever you arrived in.

For infrared you undress, because clothing blocks the light. The less you wear, the better. Underwear is fine. You're on your own in the room.

For massage you undress to your underwear and stay under towels throughout. For reflexology you take off your shoes and socks. For Indian head massage you stay seated and dressed. The therapist tells you what to take off before the treatment starts.

How long does a session take?

Twelve minutes to an hour, depending on the therapy. Most people go straight back to work afterwards.

Do I have to buy a block?

You don't have to, but we recommend it. These treatments work over a series of sessions, and a block lowers the price of each session. If you'd like to try one first, book a single session.

Can I ask questions first?

Yes. Call 083 867 2844 or send a message and we'll tell you whether a treatment suits you.
