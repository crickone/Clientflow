Common questions

Before you come in.

Do I need to be an athlete?

No. Most people who come in aren't.

What do I wear, and do I have to undress?

It depends which one. For HIFEM and for hyperbaric oxygen you stay fully clothed, in whatever you arrived in.

Infrared is the one you undress for, because clothing blocks the light. The less you wear the better; underwear is fine. You're on your own in the room.

For massage you undress to your underwear and stay under towels throughout. Reflexology is shoes and socks, and you stay seated and dressed for Indian head massage. The therapist tells you which before anything starts.

How long does a session take?

Twelve minutes to an hour, depending on the therapy. Most people go straight back to work afterwards.

Do I have to buy a block?

You don't have to, but we recommend it. These therapies work over a run of sessions, and a block lowers the price of each one. If you'd rather try one first, book a single session.

Can I ask questions first?

Yes. Call 083 867 2844 or send a message and we'll tell you whether it suits what you're after.
