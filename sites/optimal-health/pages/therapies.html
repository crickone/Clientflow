Common questions

Before you come in.

Do I need to be an athlete?

No. Most of the people who come in are not. The clinic was built for people training hard, living busy, or simply wanting to move and sleep better.

What do I wear?

Whatever you arrived in. You stay fully clothed for HIFEM and for hyperbaric oxygen. For infrared, comfortable clothing is easiest.

How long does a session take?

Between twelve minutes and an hour, depending on which one. None of them leave you needing to sit down afterwards, so a session fits inside a lunch break. The HIFEM chair is the one exception. That is muscle work, and it is better planned the way you would plan a gym session.

Do I have to buy a block?

You do not have to, but a block is what we would recommend. A single session answers one question honestly — whether you like the room and how the thing feels — and that is worth knowing before you spend anything else. It is not the question the therapies themselves answer. None of these are one-visit treatments; they are meant to be used in a run, and a block is simply how a run is paid for, at a lower price per session.

So start with one if you would rather start smaller. If you already know you want to keep going, buy the block and save yourself the arithmetic. Every price is on one page.

Can I ask questions first?

Please do. Call 083 867 2844 or send a message and we will tell you honestly whether we think it suits what you are after.
