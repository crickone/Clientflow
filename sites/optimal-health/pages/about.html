Our story

Who we are.

We're a small team based inside Inspire in Clonmel. We offer hyperbaric oxygen, infrared, HIFEM and massage.

A member of our team sets you up and stays with you for every session.

Our clients include people who train hard, people who work long weeks, and people who want to move and sleep better. You don't need to know which treatment you want before you book. We'll help you choose.

The team

Our team.

Sandra

Clinic manager · Massage and neuromuscular therapist

Sandra manages the clinic. She has worked as a massage therapist since 2016 and is qualified in massage and neuromuscular therapy.

Before joining Optimal Health, she worked for four years in the spa at the five-star Cashel Palace Hotel, as a therapist and then as spa assistant manager. She tailors each treatment to the client to get the best result for them.
