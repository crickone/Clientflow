  • No refunds

    A membership isn't refunded once it's paid for.

    That includes unused sessions and the rest of the term. If you're unsure, buy a single session or a block first.

  • The minimum

    Paying monthly runs for three months at least.

    The six-month and twelve-month prices are paid in one payment, up front, for the term they name.

  • Pausing

    It can be paused for up to three months, on medical grounds.

    One pause a year. Tell us as early as you can.

  • Rolling over

    Sessions you don't use carry into the next month, and no further.

    They don't build up over the year, so tell us if you'll be away.

  • Transferring

    A membership can be handed to somebody else, once.

    Once per membership.

    Priority on the book, and a guest pass every quarter.

    Most useful on evenings and Saturday mornings, when it's busiest.

More about the bodywork

Claiming some of it back.

Some health insurance plans pay back part of the cost of hands-on treatments. If you hold Irish Life or Laya cover, you may be able to claim back on massage, depending on your plan. If you hold VHI cover, you may be able to claim back on reflexology, depending on your plan.

It depends on your policy, so check with your provider before you book. You'll get a receipt; if your insurer needs particular details on it, tell us before you come.
