More about the bodywork

Claiming some of it back.

Some health insurance plans pay back part of the cost of hands-on treatments. If you hold Irish Life or Laya cover, you may be able to claim back on massage, depending on your plan. If you hold VHI cover, you may be able to claim back on reflexology, depending on your plan.

Whether you can, and how much, is set by your own policy and its level rather than by us, and we are not in a position to tell you what yours allows. Ring your provider before you book, name the treatment you are coming for, and ask them directly. We will give you a receipt with everything a claim form asks for.

Before you buy a block.

Can I try one session first?

Yes, and we would rather you did. Every therapy above has a single-session price for exactly that reason. Call us and we will sort one so you can see how you find it before you commit to a run of them.

Do blocks expire?

Ask us when you buy and we will tell you exactly where you stand. We would rather be clear up front than have you lose sessions you have paid for.

Can I share a block?

Talk to us. It depends on the therapy and on how the block is set up, and it is a conversation at the desk rather than a rule we can print.

Are vouchers available?
