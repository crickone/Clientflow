The terms are below. The first one is the one people tend to find out about late, so it is first.

  • No refunds

    A membership is not refunded once it is paid for.

    Not the part of a term you have not reached, and not the sessions you did not take. If you are not certain about a membership, buy a single session or a block first. Both are further up this page, and neither of them ties you to a term.

  • The minimum

    Paying monthly runs for three months at least.

    The six-month and twelve-month prices are paid in one payment, up front, for the term they name.

  • Pausing

    It can be paused for up to three months, on medical grounds.

    One pause in a year. Tell us as early as you can and we will sort it from the desk.

  • Rolling over

    Sessions you do not use carry into the next month, and no further.

    One month is the whole of it. They do not stack up over a year, so a month you are away is worth telling us about.

  • Transferring

    A membership can be handed to somebody else, once.

    Once over the life of it, whichever term you are on.

  • Being a member

    Priority on the book, and a guest pass every quarter.

    Which counts for most in the evenings and on a Saturday morning, when the room fills first.

More about the bodywork

Claiming some of it back.

Some health insurance plans pay back part of the cost of hands-on treatments. If you hold Irish Life or Laya cover, you may be able to claim back on massage, depending on your plan. If you hold VHI cover, you may be able to claim back on reflexology, depending on your plan.

Whether you can, and how much, is set by your own policy and its level, and we are not in a position to tell you what yours allows. Ring your provider before you book, name the treatment you are coming for, and ask them directly. You will get a receipt for what you paid, and if your policy needs particular details on it, tell us before you come and we will do what we can.

Yes, and we would rather you did. Every therapy above has a single-session price for exactly that reason. Call us and we will sort one so you can see how you find it before you commit to a run of them.
