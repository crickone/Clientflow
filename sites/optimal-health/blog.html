Why these read the way they do.

Recovery writing tends to promise things. We'd rather tell you what the machine is, what the process is understood to be doing, and what people who use it say. Those are three different kinds of statement, and only the first is a fact about the room, so we keep them apart.

So the verbs here are mostly “can” and “may” rather than “will”. Where something is what clients tell us, we write it as what clients tell us. Nothing on this site diagnoses anything, and nothing here is a substitute for advice from your own clinician. If you have a diagnosed condition, follow their advice and tell us about it before you book, so we can say honestly whether a session suits you.

If any of it raises a question, ring us. A phone call beats guessing from a page.
