## Week 2 · Email · Your treatment, explained

### HBOT version

**Subject:** What a hyperbaric oxygen session is like

Hi {first name},

You asked about hyperbaric oxygen, so here is what a session involves.

You sit or lie in the chamber in comfortable everyday clothes. The pressure rises slowly and you breathe oxygen for 60 minutes. You can bring a book. Phones and other electronics stay outside.

A few things help: arrive well hydrated, and skip oily lotions, hairspray and perfume beforehand. If you have a cold, we'll move your session, because congestion makes it hard to clear your ears.

If you're worried about feeling closed in, we can arrange a trial sit-in first, with no pressure. You can talk to us at any point, and we can pause.

A single session is €80. Ring 083 867 2844 to book, or reply here with any questions.

Optimal Health

### Infrared version

**Subject:** What an infrared session is like

Hi {first name},

You asked about infrared, so here is what a session involves.

You lie on a full-body infrared bed for 12 minutes, on your own in the room. It is not a sunbed: there is no UV, so no tanning or burning. The less you wear, the more of your skin the light reaches, and underwear is fine.

Most people fit it into a lunch break or before training. You go straight back to your day afterwards.

If you have a heart condition, heat intolerance, light sensitivity, or take medication that makes you sensitive to light, check with your GP first and tell us when you book.

A single session is €50. Ring 083 867 2844 to book, or reply here with any questions.

Optimal Health

### HIFEM version

**Subject:** What a HIFEM session is like

Hi {first name},

You asked about HIFEM, so here is what a session involves.

You sit fully dressed in the HIFEM chair for 30 minutes. The chair uses a pulsed electromagnetic field (PEMF). You feel it working, and you can read or rest while it runs.

There is nothing to prepare. Anyone with a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device must not use HIFEM, so tell us about any implants when you book.

A single session is €70. Ring 083 867 2844 to book, or reply here with any questions.

Optimal Health

### Massage version

**Subject:** Which massage to book

Hi {first name},

You asked about massage, so here is a quick guide to what we offer.

- Swedish: long strokes at a steady, moderate pressure. A good first booking if you're not sure.
- Deep tissue: firmer pressure, agreed with you before and during the session.
- Sports: firmer massage and stretching on the areas your training works hardest.
- Reflexology: pressure on the feet. Only your shoes and socks come off.
- Hot lava shells: massage using warmed shells as well as hands, so you get heat and pressure together.

Most are 60 minutes, and Swedish, deep tissue and sports also come as 30-minute sessions from €50.

Irish Life and Laya may pay back part of a massage, and VHI part of reflexology, depending on your plan. We'll give you a receipt.

Ring 083 867 2844 to book, or reply here and tell us what you'd like help with.

## Week 8 · Email · Common questions

**Subject:** Questions people ask us

Hi {first name},

The questions we're asked most, answered.

**Is it a medical treatment?** No. These are wellness sessions. We don't diagnose or treat medical conditions. If you're under a doctor's care, follow their advice and tell us before you book.

**What do I wear?** Everyday clothes for HBOT and HIFEM. Underwear is fine for infrared. For massage you lie under towels.

**Can I try one session first?** Yes. Every therapy has a single-session price. Blocks cost less per session.

**Can I bring someone?** Ring us and we'll arrange it.

**Can I combine therapies or train the same day?** Yes, many people do. We'll help you space them out.

**Who should check with a GP first?** Anyone pregnant, and anyone with ear, sinus, heart or breathing conditions, implanted devices or recent surgery. Tell us when you book.

Anything else, reply here or ring 083 867 2844.
